Vylroh Co., Ltd. Established

We are pleased to announce the formal establishment of Vylroh Co., Ltd., headquartered at R-cube Aoyama, Minato, Tokyo. Capitalised at ¥7,000,000, Vylroh was founded by Representative Director Hiroshi Kawamoto with the mission of building digital tools that help people live more healthfully — and more fully.

Our initial portfolio spans healthcare navigation for international travelers in Japan, clinical wellness applications, and tools for enriching everyday life. Further product announcements will follow in the months ahead.
