Vylroh Co., Ltd. Established

We are pleased to announce the establishment of Vylroh Co., Ltd., headquartered in Kita-Aoyama, Minato, Tokyo. Vylroh is a healthcare development company connecting real-world clinical practice with medical innovation, built on the founder's experience as both a practicing clinician and a medical development professional.

We will be advancing development strategy consulting for pharmaceutical companies and CROs, clinical AI and clinic DX systems, SaMD-oriented digital health platforms, and traveler medical infrastructure. Further announcements will follow in the months ahead.
