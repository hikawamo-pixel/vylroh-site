July 12, 2026

Constipation Care Platform (First Release) Launched at Fujimi Clinic

Vylroh Co., Ltd. has launched the first release of its constipation care platform at Fujimi Clinic (Ota, Tokyo). The web app lets patients record their daily condition themselves and share it with their physician at the time of consultation.

It also serves as an electronic patient-reported outcome (ePRO) tool for constipation clinical trials. Going forward, we will progressively add treatment-supporting features and advance development toward a Software as a Medical Device (SaMD).

An English version of the web app is currently in development.

July 7, 2026

Tokyo Traveler Medical Platform Now Live

Vylroh Co., Ltd. is pleased to announce the official launch of Tokyo Traveler Medical (tokyo-traveler-medical.com), a medical platform for international visitors to Japan.

Powered by Fujimi Clinic in Ota, Tokyo, the platform offers English-language online consultations, in-clinic visits, and house calls — combined into one seamless experience. Pediatric and adult care are available, with care provided by board-certified pediatricians.

Hotel and travel operator partnerships, as well as physician network expansion, are planned for the months ahead.

May 7, 2026

Vylroh Co., Ltd. Established

We are pleased to announce the establishment of Vylroh Co., Ltd., headquartered in Kita-Aoyama, Minato, Tokyo. Vylroh is a healthcare development company connecting real-world clinical practice with medical innovation, built on the founder's experience as both a practicing clinician and a medical development professional.

We will be advancing development strategy consulting for pharmaceutical companies and CROs, clinical AI and clinic DX systems, SaMD-oriented digital health platforms, and traveler medical infrastructure. Further announcements will follow in the months ahead.
